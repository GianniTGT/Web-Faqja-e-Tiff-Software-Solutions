Privacy

Almost nothing, and here is exactly what.

A short page, because this is a short answer. Every line below is a statement about how the site is built, not a promise about how it is used.

Does this site use cookies?

No. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. That is also why there is no cookie banner — there is nothing to ask you about.

Is there analytics or tracking?

No. No analytics, no tag manager, no advertising pixel, no session recording. We do not know which pages you looked at.

Does anything load from another company?

No. The typefaces are served from this domain rather than from Google, so opening a page here does not send your address to anybody else. There are no embedded maps, fonts, videos or scripts from third parties.

What about the server?

The hosting provider keeps ordinary web-server logs — your IP address, the page requested, the time, your browser — for a short period, for security and to keep the server running. That is technically necessary and it is not used to build a profile of you.

If you email us

Your message and your address sit in our mailbox, and we use them to answer you and to carry on the conversation if there is one. They are not added to a mailing list, not passed to anybody, and not sold. Ask us to delete the correspondence and we will.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Write to info@tiff-software-solutions.com and you will get an answer from a person. There is no form and no ticket number.

If this changes

The day this site gains analytics, a contact form or anything embedded from another company, this page changes in the same commit. It is part of the site, not a document filed away from it.
